Rules

Draft

Fill six headliner slots. Each round rolls a genre and decade and presents six artists. Choose exactly one artist to lock into your lineup.

Win condition

Your six picks produce a final festival score. Reach S tier at 93+ to win.

FDC BAS

Hard Mode

Enable it before starting. Numerical card stats, Headliner Score and live lineup score are hidden during the draft; qualitative archetypes remain as clues. Full numbers return after the draft.

Head-to-Head

Two players share each six-card spin and draft one artist each. The first picker alternates each round. The match shares one decade reroll and one genre reroll, usable only before the first pick of a round.

Rerolls

You get two independent one-use rerolls. Decade Reroll keeps the genre and changes the decade. Genre Reroll keeps the decade and changes the genre.

Stats

Every artist has Live, Hits, Influence, Buzz, Critic, and Catalog ratings. Headliner Score weights them at 22%, 17%, 18%, 13%, 15%, and 15% respectively.

Rarity

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary. Rarity controls how likely an artist is to appear in a spin. It does not directly determine their stats.

Diversity bonus

Mixing genres and decades adds up to +4 points to the final lineup score, rewarding a broader festival without overpowering artist quality.
